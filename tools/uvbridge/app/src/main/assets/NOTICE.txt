UV Bridge
=========

A companion Android app for the F4HWN Edition Fusion firmware for the
Quansheng UV-K1 and UV-K5 V3:

  https://github.com/armel/uv-k1-k5v3-firmware-custom

UV Bridge is developed by Phoenix1o1 in tools/uvbridge of
https://github.com/Phoenix1o1/uv-k1-k5v3-firmware-custom and is licensed
under the Apache License, Version 2.0, like the firmware project
(licenses/Apache-2.0.txt).

It is not affiliated with Quansheng. Quansheng, UV-K1 and UV-K5 are
names of their respective owners.

F4HWN firmware
--------------

The serial programming protocol, the firmware asset pack format (FWA1 /
FWAT) and the asset commands used by UV Bridge are those of the F4HWN
firmware by Armel FAUVEAU (F4HWN), muzkr and contributors, based on the
firmwares of Dual Tachyon and Egzumer, licensed under the Apache License,
Version 2.0 (see the NOTICE file of the firmware project).


CHIRP and the F4HWN CHIRP driver
--------------------------------

The CHIRP image format, and the way channels and settings are stored,
follow CHIRP and its F4HWN driver for the UV-K1 & UV-K5 V3 (F4HWN), by
F4HWN, JOC2 and Egzumer, based on the UV-K5 driver by Jacek Lipkowski
(SQ5BPF) and on CHIRP by Dan Smith and contributors. CHIRP and the
driver are licensed under the GNU General Public License.

  https://chirpmyradio.com/
  https://github.com/armel/uv-k1-k5v3-firmware-custom/releases

UV Bridge does not include CHIRP or any driver file. Driver files are
downloaded from the firmware releases (or loaded) at the user's request;
the app reads their memory map and setting lists and never runs them.
